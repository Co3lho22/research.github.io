Master's Thesis

A Human-in-the-Loop Security Control Validation Framework for Data Exfiltration Detection

concluded
INESC TEC CSIRT U.Porto Networking Team U.Porto detection engineering

Goal

Security teams struggle to know whether their detection controls actually work — and when they don't, whether it's a configuration problem, a gap in coverage, or a missed attack pattern. This thesis builds a framework that puts a human analyst in the loop: systematically stress-testing security controls against data exfiltration scenarios and surfacing where they need to be tuned.

Why it matters

Most detection validation happens reactively — after an incident. This framework flips that: it gives security teams a structured way to continuously probe their own defences, identify blind spots before attackers do, and build confidence that their controls hold under realistic conditions. The human-in-the-loop design means the results are actionable, not just another automated report.
